## Full description (max 4000) — 1621 chars
Learn the 3,000 most important American English words — with meanings, examples, pictures and audio in your own language.

3000 Words follows the Oxford 3000 word list from level A1 to B2: the words that cover most of everyday English. Lessons are short, the app works offline, and the interface speaks 31 languages, including Persian, Arabic, Spanish, German, Russian, Turkish, Portuguese, French, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

HOW IT WORKS
• Short lessons: see each word with a picture, an example sentence and audio, then practise it three times.
• Smart review: words come back after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days so they stick.
• Hard words: the words you miss are collected so you can train them.
• 60-second challenge: how many words can you get right?
• Placement test: find your level in two minutes and start where it makes sense.
• Daily streak and an optional daily reminder.

YOUR PROGRESS, EVERYWHERE
Use the app without an account, or create a free account to keep your progress safe and sync it across your devices. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.
