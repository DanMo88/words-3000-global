Privacy policy

English 3000 Words (“3000 Words”, the web app at danmo88.github.io/words-3000-global and its Android app) — developer: Daniel Mozafari. Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Short version: no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data. Without an account everything stays on your device. With an account we store your email, name and learning progress so you can use them on all your devices. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Who is responsible

Daniel Mozafari, the developer of the app (“we”). Contact: mozafar.nl@gmail.com

Without an account (guest)

Your chosen languages, learning progress, streak, scores and settings (including a daily reminder time) are stored only in your browser’s local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

  • Email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider) — to sign you in. If you use “Continue with Google”, we receive your email address and name from Google.
  • Display name (optional) and your native language.
  • Learning progress per language course: which words you learned and when, review schedule, completed lessons, streak, best score in the 60-second game and placement-test result.
  • Purchase record if you buy Premium: the order number, status (paid/refunded), amount, currency, product and the email used at checkout, as sent to us by Lemon Squeezy. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Device record for the one-device limit of Premium: a random device ID created by the app on your device (not a hardware ID), a short label read from your browser (for example “Android · Chrome”), which device is active, when each device was last seen and when Premium was last moved.
  • Deletion requests sent through the form on the delete-account page: the email address and the optional note.

We use this data only to provide the app: sign-in, syncing your progress between devices, unlocking Premium on your active device, and handling your requests. Legal basis (GDPR): performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where the data is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase (database and login service) in the European Union, Frankfurt (AWS eu-central-1). The app’s files are hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy (Lemon Squeezy LLC, a Stripe company), our reseller and merchant of record: it collects your name, email, billing country and payment details itself to take the payment, prevent fraud, send the receipt and handle taxes, under the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy; Lemon Squeezy and its payment processors may process this data outside the EU (e.g. in the USA) with appropriate safeguards. The payment window (lemon.js) is loaded from Lemon Squeezy only when you tap “Buy”. We do not use any other third-party services, advertising networks, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not use cookies ourselves (only local storage needed for the app to work; the Lemon Squeezy payment window may use its own cookies while you pay).

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your login, profile, synced progress, purchase records and device records (Lemon Squeezy keeps its own order records as required by tax law); progress saved on your own device stays there until you clear it. Requests sent through the delete-account form are handled within 30 days. Data can remain in our providers’ encrypted backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

Your rights

You can see and change your name in the app, and delete your account in the app (👤 → Delete account) or on the delete-account page. You also have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us at the address above.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the time is stored only on your device and your browser or Android asks for permission to show notifications. We do not use a push server.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool. If you are under 16, please create an account only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

  • درخواست‌های حذف که از فرم صفحه‌ی حذف حساب فرستاده می‌شوند: ایمیل و توضیح اختیاری.

این داده‌ها فقط برای کارکرد اپ استفاده می‌شوند: ورود، همگام‌سازی پیشرفت بین دستگاه‌ها، فعال کردن پریمیوم روی دستگاه فعالت و رسیدگی به درخواست‌هایت. مبنای قانونی (GDPR): اجرای قرارداد با تو (ماده‌ی ۶(۱)(ب)) و منافع مشروع ما در امن نگه داشتن سرویس (ماده‌ی ۶(۱)(و)).
